🚗 Option B: ~1 hr to Guanella Pass   Open in Maps ↗
Guanella Pass / Mount Blue Sky 2–3 hrs driving + exploring
Paved scenic byway to 11,669ft. AWD optional but comfortable. Mount Blue Sky (formerly Mt Evans) is one of the highest paved roads in North America — Summit at 14,130ft. Views are absurd. Check road open status before going.
