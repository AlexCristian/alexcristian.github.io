Day 1 Friday, June 19 · Boulder
Arrive in Boulder ~9:00am
Uber from airport, settle in, grab coffee. Check into Airbnb.
Arrival
🏠 Book Airbnb in: Central Boulder (Pearl Street / University Hill area) — easy walking to restaurants and trailheads. Backup: North Boulder near Chautauqua.
🥞 9:00am — Breakfast / Coffee · ~1hr
🚗 ~10 min · 2.5 mi   Open in Maps ↗
Chautauqua Park & Flatirons 10:30am – 12:30pm
Iconic Boulder hike with front-row Flatirons views. Chautauqua Trail → Bluebell Mesa loop is the classic. Moderate, well-marked, solid for a group.
Hike Scenic 🥾 AllTrails ↗
Royal Arch Trailharder — 3.3mi, 1,400ft gain to a dramatic natural rock arch. Much more of a workout than the loop, big payoff at the top. Same trailhead.
Green Mountain Summitlonger — 5mi loop, 2,200ft gain, true 360° summit panorama. Less crowded than Chautauqua. Park on Flagstaff Rd.
Eldorado Canyon State Parkscenic — 20min south of Boulder. World-class climbing walls, gorgeous narrow canyon, easy canyon trail for non-climbers. Free to walk in.
🚶 ~15 min walk / 5 min drive   Open in Maps ↗
Boulder Dushanbe Teahouse 1:00pm – 2:30pm
Handcrafted Persian teahouse — gift from Boulder's sister city in Tajikistan. Good lunch / late lunch. Book a reservation ahead if possible (especially weekend).
Lunch Unique
Frasca Food & Wineupscale — James Beard-nominated Italian on Pearl Street. Dinner reservation needed weeks ahead. One of the best meals in Colorado.
The Kitchencasual — Farm-to-table, very Boulder. Good for a group lunch, right on Pearl Street.
Avery Brewing / Rayback Collectivebeer — If you'd rather skip a sit-down meal and grab craft beer + food trucks. Rayback has outdoor space, great vibes.
🚗 ~1 hr 30 min · Idaho Springs   Open in Maps ↗
Clear Creek Whitewater Rafting 4:00pm – 6:00pm
Class III–IV whitewater on Clear Creek through a dramatic granite canyon. 2hr trip, ~$60–80/person. Several outfitters run afternoon trips: Clear Creek Rafting, Arkansas Valley Adventures, or Blazing Adventures. Book ahead — afternoon slots fill. Idaho Springs is a cute old mining town; grab a beer at Tommyknocker Brewery after.
Rafting Class III–IV
Arkansas River (Buena Vista)harder — Class IV–V on the Numbers or Browns Canyon. 2.5hr drive from Boulder. Better whitewater but a much longer day — consider swapping all of Day 2 for this instead.
Breckenridge afternoonmellow — 1.5hr from Boulder. High-altitude ski town in summer: gondola, hiking, great food. Skip rafting and do this if the group wants chill over adrenaline.
Stay in Bouldereasy day — Skip the drive entirely. More time on Pearl Street, bike the creek path, hit Avery Brewing. Perfectly valid if legs are tired from the morning hike.
🚗 ~1 hr back to Boulder   Open in Maps ↗
Pearl Street Mall 7:00pm – 8:00pm
Walkable outdoor mall with shops, street performers, good coffee. Chill afternoon vibe.
Explore
Boulder Creek Pathactive — 5mi paved path along Boulder Creek. Rent bikes at University Bicycles (~$35/day), cruise toward the mountains. Good option if you want to keep moving.
CU Boulder campus walkfree — Beautiful Tuscan-style sandstone buildings, one of the best-looking campuses in the US. 10min walk from Pearl St.
Celestial Seasonings factory tourquirky — Free tour + tasting room of the tea brand. The peppermint room is genuinely overwhelming. Weirdly fun, ~45min.

Day 2 Saturday, June 20 · Rocky Mountain National Park
☕ 6:45am — Early breakfast · ~45 min · Leave by 7:30am (RMNP gets busy fast)
🚗 ~1 hr 15 min · 42 mi   Open in Maps ↗
Bear Lake → Dream Lake → Emerald Lake 8:45am – 11:30am
The classic RMNP loop. Bear Lake (easy, 0.6mi) → Nymph Lake → Dream Lake → Emerald Lake. Round trip ~3.6mi, 605ft gain. Stunning alpine scenery. TIMED ENTRY PERMIT required — buy in advance at recreation.gov.
Hike Alpine Lakes 🥾 AllTrails ↗
Sky Pond via Glacier Gorgeharder — 9.5mi, 1,800ft gain. More demanding than Emerald Lake, fewer crowds, ends at a wild glacial tarn below Taylor Peak. Full day hike — skip Estes if you do this.
Twin Sisters Peaksno permit — 7.4mi, 2,300ft, east side of RMNP. No timed entry permit needed. Solid summit views of Longs Peak and the park.
Trail Ridge Roadscenic drive — Highest continuous paved US highway at 12,183ft. Drive through the entire park instead of hiking — 48mi, takes 2–3hrs with stops. Great if energy is low after Day 1.
Arkansas River Rafting (Buena Vista)swap whole day — 2.5hr drive from Boulder. Class IV–V whitewater on the Numbers or Browns Canyon. Half-day or full-day trips. If the group wants adrenaline over alpine lakes, this is the move for Day 2. Book ahead.
🥙 11:30am — Packed lunch / snacks at trailhead or Estes Park · ~1hr
🚗 ~15 min · 8 mi   Open in Maps ↗
📍 12:30pm — Estes Park · Pick 1 or both (2–3 hrs total)
  • Stanley Hotel — The Shining filming location. Tour or just walk the grounds. Creepy good.
  • Estes Park Aerial Tramway — ~8min ride, panoramic views of the valley + peaks. Open daily.
  • Lake Estes trail — Easy 3.75mi flat loop around the reservoir, great views of the peaks. Good cool-down after the morning hike.
🚗 3:30pm — Depart Estes Park
🚗 ~1 hr 15 min back to Boulder   Open in Maps ↗
🍻 6:30pm — Dinner + drinks · ~1.5 hrs
Sunset on Flagstaff Mountain 8:00pm – 9:15pm
Best sunset view in Boulder — drive 10 minutes up Flagstaff Road, park at Realization Point, and watch the Flatirons turn orange against the entire Front Range. Sunset is ~8:35pm in late June; the golden hour buildup is the real show. Minimal effort after a big hiking day — this is a sit-and-soak-it-in stop. Bring a jacket, it's breezy up top.
Sunset Views
Pearl Street Evening Strolleasy — The mall comes alive after dark: street performers, patio bars, ice cream. No driving needed from your Airbnb. Best option if everyone's legs are completely done.
Avery Brewing / Rayback Collectivebeer — World-class Colorado craft beer: Avery's taproom (~15min drive north) or the Rayback outdoor food truck park in town. Perfect low-key end to a big mountain day.
🌙 10:00pm — Sleep · Boulder Airbnb
Day 3 Sunday, June 21 · Colorado Springs · ✈️ Midnight flight
☕ 8:00am — Breakfast + check out · ~1 hr
🚗 ~45 min to Idaho Springs   Open in Maps ↗
Indian Hot Springs (Idaho Springs) 9:00am – 11:30am
Geothermal mineral pools just off I-70 — a perfect recovery stop before the drive south. Indoor cave baths + outdoor pools. $25–35/person. On the way to Colorado Springs.
Hot Springs Relax
Clear Creek Whitewater Rafting🌊 active — Same canyon, same exit. Multiple outfitters in Idaho Springs (Clear Creek Rafting, Whitewater Excitement). Class III–IV, 1.5–3hr trips ~$50–70/person. Perfect swap if the group wants adrenaline over soaking. Book morning slot to still make Colorado Springs.
Georgetown Loop Railroadhistoric — 6.5mi narrow-gauge steam train loop over the Devil's Gate bridge, ~1hr ride. Right in Georgetown, 10min from Idaho Springs. Fun quick detour.
Guanella Pass scenic drivefree — Head up from Georgetown instead of hot springs. Paved AWD road to 11,669ft with access to Mount Blue Sky (14,130ft). Free, dramatic, 1–2hr round trip.
🚗 ~1 hr 30 min · 90 mi   Open in Maps ↗
Garden of the Gods 1:00pm – 3:00pm
Massive red sandstone formations against Pikes Peak backdrop. Free to enter. Drive the loop or walk the main paved path — either works well for a group. One of the best things in Colorado, full stop.
Scenic Walk 🥾 AllTrails ↗
Cave of the Winds Mountain Parkadventurous — 20min drive. Cave tours, Via Ferrata routes, wind walker suspension bridge. Good option if the group wants more activity after Garden of the Gods.
Seven Fallsscenic — 181ft waterfall in a box canyon, accessible via short trail. ~15min from Garden of Gods. Pairs well as a quick add-on.
US Air Force Academyfree — Visitor center + Cadet Chapel (one of the most architecturally striking buildings in the US). Free, on the way to Manitou Springs.
🚗 ~10 min · 4 mi   Open in Maps ↗
Manitou Springs + Cliff Dwellings 3:15pm – 5:30pm
Quirky mountain town at the foot of Pikes Peak. Walk the main strip, try the natural mineral springs fountains (free, scattered around town). Manitou Cliff Dwellings nearby — authentic Ancestral Puebloan structures, $12/person.
Town History
Manitou Inclinebrutal — 2,744 steps, 2,000ft gain in under a mile. Local legend. Takes ~1hr up, 30min down. Free. For the group that wants to suffer in a good way.
North Cheyenne Cañon Parkfree — Beautiful canyon just south of town. Helen Hunt Falls is an easy 15min walk. Serious trails branch off for longer hikes.
Pikes Peak Cog Railwayfull afternoon — Departs from Manitou Springs. 3.5hr round trip, $40/person. Book well ahead — sells out. Swap the cliff dwellings if you go this route.

Day 4 Monday, June 22 · Group B only
☕ 9:00am — Breakfast in Glenwood Springs · ~1 hr
Glenwood Hot Springs Pool 10:00am – 12:30pm
One of the world's largest outdoor mineral hot springs pools — two pools, 405ft long, set in a canyon with cliffs on both sides. $30/person. The perfect recovery from a long weekend. Been running since 1888. Vapor Caves below the pool are also worth adding (~$18 extra) — natural geothermal steam caves carved into the rock.
Hot Springs Canyon
🚗 ~40 min · Glenwood Springs → Aspen   Open in Maps ↗
Aspen + Maroon Bells 1:30pm – 3:15pm
Maroon Bells — the most photographed peaks in North America. Twin 14,000ft summits reflected in Maroon Lake. Easy 1.8mi loop trail around the lake, or just the viewpoint. Shuttle required in summer — take the Roaring Fork Transit Authority bus from Aspen (~$8 roundtrip, runs hourly). Back in Aspen: great town for a quick lunch/coffee — try Cache Cache for French bistro or grab a pastry at Jour de Fête. Aspen is fancy but walkable and the mountains are free. Note: with a 9pm DEN flight, leave Aspen by 3:30pm — that gives you 3.5hrs of drive time and a comfortable 2hr airport buffer.
