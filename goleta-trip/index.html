Day 6 Saturday, July 4 · 4th of July — Hike + Beach + Fireworks
☕ 6:30am — Early breakfast + trail prep · ~30 min
🚗 Leave ~7:00am · ~20 min · Inspiration Point Trailhead, Tunnel Rd   Open in Maps ↗
Inspiration Point 7:20am – 10:00am
3.5mi round trip, ~935ft gain, ~2–2.5 hours. Starts at the Tunnel Trail trailhead on Tunnel Rd (north of Santa Barbara Botanic Garden — roadside parking). The climb goes through oak woodland and chaparral to a ridgeline saddle with panoramic views of Santa Barbara, the coast, and the Channel Islands on one side, the Santa Ynez Valley on the other. Start at 7:20am to finish before the July heat sets in and before the trail gets crowded. No permit required, free. ⚠️ Verify trail is open: Tunnel Trail was closed for up to 4 months starting Sep 2025 for Southern California Edison restoration work. May be open by Jul 2026 — confirm at the Los Padres NF ranger station before visiting.

🚗 Leave ~10:00am · ~15 min · East Beach, Santa Barbara   Open in Maps ↗
East Beach BBQ — 4th of July 10:30am – 8:30pm
East Beach is Santa Barbara's widest, best-maintained beach — long stretch of sand along Cabrillo Blvd with volleyball courts and a dedicated BBQ/picnic area. Claim a charcoal grill spot early (they fill by noon on the 4th). Supplies: grab from Von's or Trader Joe's the night before — burgers, chicken, corn, watermelon, drinks. The beach fills through the afternoon as the fireworks crowd arrives; set up your blanket and chairs for the evening. You're in a good viewing position right here on East Beach — no need to move for fireworks. Note: alcohol policy in picnic areas varies by permit — verify current rules at the beach entrance.

Carpinteria State Beachmellow — ~20 min south on US-101. Known as one of the world's safest beaches (extremely gentle surf). Smaller 4th crowd, more local family vibe. Good if you want a quieter beach day.
4th of July Fireworks — West Beach 9:00pm – 9:45pm
Fireworks launch from West Beach along Cabrillo Blvd — if you've been at East Beach all day, you're already in a great position (both beaches connect along the same waterfront). Show starts ~9pm, lasts ~20 minutes. Free, no tickets. Tune to KJEE 92.9 FM for the synchronized soundtrack. Road closures on Cabrillo Blvd start at 6pm — plan your car accordingly. Post-show traffic: allow 20–30 min to exit the area. Waterfront parking is $20 for the event.

☕ 7:30am — Early breakfast + pack · ~30 min
🚗 Leave ~8:00am · ~15 min · Rattlesnake Canyon Trailhead, Las Canoas Rd   Open in Maps ↗
Rattlesnake Canyon Trail 8:15am – 11:30am
5.1mi round-trip, ~1,000–1,584ft gain, ~3–3.5 hours — this is a real hike, not a stroll. Trailhead: take Mission Canyon Rd north from SB, right on Las Canoas Rd, park near the narrow bridge before Skofield Park (look for the wooden trailhead sign). The trail follows a year-round creek through a shaded canyon of oaks and sycamores — multiple creek crossings, manageable in July. Rattlesnake Canyon is reliably one of the best hikes in the area: enclosed, cool, and surprisingly wild for being 15 minutes from downtown. No permit required, free. Finish by ~11:30am for brunch with your friends before they head out.
